# The daily duties log (pirate ship; Rex, 2026-10-26)

Part of the pirate-ship spec (`PIRATE.md`, §5 and §5a). Every daily duty on the ship, so the morning is Stardew's
"what do I tend today" and the ship feels like a real one. **Each duty gets a minigame, and the minigames are
Rex's to design**: the Minigame column stays empty until he fills it. Appendix B of `PIRATE.md` has scaffold ideas
for most of these, for reference only.

Columns: **Real practice** is what crews of the period (17th–18th century) actually did, so the duty is
authentic. **Keeps** is the ship part from §5a. **If skipped** is what grows each day it's left. **Uses** is the
supply. **Skill** is what doing it raises (proposed skill names below). Everything except Rex's sail example is
proposed.

## Skills (proposed)

Seamanship (sails, rigging, the helm) · Carpentry (hull, repairs) · Navigation · Fishing · Cooking · Gunnery ·
Surgery · Gambling. As in Stardew, a skill's levels unlock things: recipes, better repairs, new tables, new duties.

**Crew mates and minigames (Rex, 2026-10-26).** A crew mate in a post handles that duty's minigame for you, so
the duty gets done without you (the Stardew sprinkler). You can still join them: fish with the fisherman and you
play the minigame, improve your relationship with him, and add to his catch for the day, which makes his duties
lighter. Minigames are never required; playing them speeds up progress.

How well a crew mate does a duty alone is their performance (`STATS.md` §4: skill, attributes, mood, madness).
The ISTJ bosun swabs the deck perfectly and flogs whoever tracks mud on it.

## Events that start a morning (proposed, Rex's sail tear the model)

You wake up and: a sail is torn (Rex) · water in the hold is up a foot · rats got into the biscuit · a cask has
burst · a goat is loose · the powder got damp · someone is sick · a crew mate is in the brig · a sail on the
horizon · a storm coming · two crew mates fought in the night · something of yours is missing.

## In port only (later builds)

Careening (the ship hauled onto a beach and the bottom scraped; slow and dangerous, the ship helpless) · buying
supplies (cloth, rope, pitch, food, rum, powder) · selling cargo and plunder · recruiting · the tavern tables.
