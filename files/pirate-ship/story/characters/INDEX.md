its archetypes and jobs are **proposed** until Rex keeps them, and Rex has said the draft reads his abstractions
too literally. No names yet: names are Rex's.

A slot gets a file in this folder (`<slug>.md`, from `_TEMPLATE.md`) once Rex makes the character.
