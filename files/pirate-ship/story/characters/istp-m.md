### His arc (Rex, 2026-10-26)

- **(Rex)** He is currently trying to find bigger and bigger fish. His goal is to hunt down dangerous prey: to
  find the biggest and most dangerous thing.
- **(Rex) The shark scene:** he jumps into the water to harpoon a shark, because he sees no other option than
  getting into the water with it.
- **(Rex) In the water with him:** you feel he could kill you at any moment; you're in his element and in a
  weakened position.
- **(Rex, aside)** There might eventually be a land-hunting game; not added yet.
- **(open)** Where the hunt ends: what the biggest, most dangerous thing is, and what happens when he finds it.

### Proposed (outside AI, 2026-10-26; not canon)

- He doesn't see himself as a killer but as a hunter; killing is the natural end of a good hunt, with no line
  between hunting animals and hunting people. Not sadistic, not trying to scare, not proving anything.
- His prey escalates alongside the player's fishing: big fish, then sharks, then sea creatures sailors won't
  approach. Money doesn't move him (he'd refuse 500 gold for a harmless fish); "sailors are vanishing near a reef"
  does. A great fisherman and an unreliable employee: he drops a paying job for a harder target.
- The shark scene staged: something huge takes the bait on a small boat, the line snaps, he picks up the harpoon,
  "Line won't hold," and goes in; the water turns red; he resurfaces. A practical answer to bad gear, not courage.
- In the water: quiet on land, completely at ease below. Lines like "You're kicking too much. Everything down there
  knows where you are." and "If I wanted to catch you, you wouldn't see me coming," said without hostility.
- The flaw: he can't leave a worthy hunt unfinished. Something he can't kill, he studies, re-gears and returns to.
  Stages: bigger fish → sharks and predators → something he can't hunt → obsession, neglecting duties → the hunt
  endangers you or the crew → your relationship shapes his choice. Endings: restraint, the hunt of his life with
  your help, or the obsession consumes him.
- The romance: affection shown as inclusion in more dangerous hunts (a liability → taught to track → trusted to
  watch his back). The darker read: you become the most interesting prey he's met; love, admiration and the
  thrill of someone who might hunt him back overlap.
- The town: its fishermen despise him for hunting what should be left alone, drawing predators onto the fishing
  grounds. Recruiting him might make the port safer, or leave it without its best defence against sea predators.
- Tone: he never acts like a horror character (no sinister smiles, threats or predator speeches); he talks like a
  professional about his trade. Contrast with the ENTP: the ENTP frightens by being erratic, he by being
  methodical and at ease with violence.
