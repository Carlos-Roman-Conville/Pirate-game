### Her arc (Rex, 2026-10-26)

- **(Rex)** She is currently trying to find bigger and bigger fish. Her goal is to hunt down dangerous prey: to
  find the biggest and most dangerous thing.
- **(Rex) The shark scene:** she jumps into the water to harpoon a shark, because she sees no other option than
  getting into the water with it.
- **(Rex) In the water with her:** you feel she could kill you at any moment; you're in her element and in a
  weakened position.
- **(Rex, aside)** There might eventually be a land-hunting game; not added yet.
- **(open)** Where the hunt ends: what the biggest, most dangerous thing is, and what happens when she finds it.

### Proposed (outside AI, 2026-10-26; not canon; written when she was a man)

- She doesn't see herself as a killer but as a hunter; killing is the natural end of a good hunt, with no line
  between hunting animals and hunting people. Not sadistic, not trying to scare, not proving anything.
- Her prey escalates alongside the player's fishing: big fish, then sharks, then sea creatures sailors won't
  approach. Money doesn't move her (she'd refuse 500 gold for a harmless fish); "sailors are vanishing near a reef"
  does. A great fisherman and an unreliable employee: she drops a paying job for a harder target.
- The shark scene staged: something huge takes the bait on a small boat, the line snaps, she picks up the harpoon,
  "Line won't hold," and goes in; the water turns red; she resurfaces. A practical answer to bad gear, not courage.
- In the water: quiet on land, completely at ease below. Lines like "You're kicking too much. Everything down there
  knows where you are." and "If I wanted to catch you, you wouldn't see me coming," said without hostility.
- The flaw: she can't leave a worthy hunt unfinished. Something she can't kill, she studies, re-gears and returns to.
  Stages: bigger fish → sharks and predators → something she can't hunt → obsession, neglecting duties → the hunt
  endangers you or the crew → your relationship shapes her choice. Endings: restraint, the hunt of her life with
  your help, or the obsession consumes her.
- The romance: affection shown as inclusion in more dangerous hunts (a liability → taught to track → trusted to
  watch her back). The darker read: you become the most interesting prey she's met; love, admiration and the
  thrill of someone who might hunt her back overlap.
- The town: its fishermen despise her for hunting what should be left alone, drawing predators onto the fishing
  grounds. Recruiting her might make the port safer, or leave it without its best defence against sea predators.
- Tone: she never acts like a horror character (no sinister smiles, threats or predator speeches); she talks like a
  professional about her trade. Contrast with the ENTP: the ENTP frightens by being erratic, she by being
  methodical and at ease with violence.
