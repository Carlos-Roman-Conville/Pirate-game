# Judging log

One dated entry each time Rex asks how he's doing, newest first. Each entry says what's working and what isn't,
specifically and honestly, judged against Rex's own goals (from `STORY.md`):

1. Hot psychos that a pirate setting makes permissible.
2. Dangerous in different ways.
3. Dating doesn't fix them.
4. An interconnected crew.

Entry shape: date, what was judged (files and versions), working, not working, the one thing to fix first.

## 2026-10-27: the cast so far

**Judged:** the 8 drafted characters (`entp-m`, `istp-m`, `isfj-f`, `istj-m`, `estp-m`, `intp-f`, `esfp-m`,
`intj-f`), the ESTJ and ENFJ maps, the bigwig and child, `QUESTLINES.md`.

**Working**

- **Dangerous in different ways (goal 2): strong.** Each danger is a different kind of danger: an erratic tinkerer
  of torture devices, a calm predator who is at home in the water, a carer who savours the pain she causes, a
  priest cruel by the letter, an old killer behind a locked door, a cannibal who feeds the town, a researcher who
  experiments on people she judges evil. None overlap.
- **The types are doing real work.** The ENTP who can't commit, the ISTP who hunts perfection, and especially the
  INTJ map (every input confirms the vision; only being proven wrong moves her) read as the type, not as a
  costume. The alignment maps are becoming a way to express how each mind works.
- **The ENTP arc is the best thing here.** Earning his loyalty costs the town, which delivers goal 3 (closeness
  doesn't fix him, it just redirects him) and ties a romance-side choice to the main plot.
- **Several characters plug straight into the dying port.** The apothecary's tests, the weaponsmith, the baker who
  feeds everyone, the farmer and the soldier at the fort are things the town depends on, so losing or changing one
  costs the port something.

**Not working yet**

- **Interconnected crew (goal 4): empty.** No character has a relationship with any other. The only links are the
  cook looking like a first mate who doesn't exist yet, and the bigwig and his child. Right now it's eight solo
  acts sharing a map.
- **"Permissible because pirates" (goal 1) is thin for some.** It fits the hunter, the weaponsmith and the
  apothecary, whose work a criminal haven would tolerate or pay for. The baker's cannibalism and the doctor's
  sadism are monstrous in any setting, so the pirate world isn't what licenses them. The `permission` field is
  empty on every file.
- **"Hot" isn't on the page yet.** No character has anything on attraction, flirting or what courting them looks
  like. Only two have a heart scene (the ENTP's departure, the ISTP's shark). The ESTP is "old": is he
  romanceable?
- **The secret-monster reveal is repeating.** The doctor, the baker and partly the farmer are all pleasant fronts
  over a hidden horror. Three of the same reveal in one small town will start to feel like a formula.
- **The INTP farmer is the thinnest.** "Psychopathic in some capacity" plus a reference character is a look and a
  voice, not a danger yet.

**Fix first:** the web. Give each character at least one tie to another resident (rival, debt, secret, past
romance). It's the goal with nothing in it, and it's what makes the questline reactions matter, since they react
to what they see.
