# The crew roster: 32 crew mates (Rex's idea, 2026-10-26; draft v0.1)

Part of the pirate-ship spec (`../PIRATE.md`, §7). Characters live here, not in High Roller's data.

**The plan (Rex):** 32 crew mates, a man and a woman for each of the 16 types, so the game plays the same for any
player. Each one fuses two things: the **killer-psycho version of their type** and a **pirate archetype** that fits
it. The pirate setting makes the psycho side something the world allows: piracy already runs on marooning,
floggings, boarding, wrecking and plunder, so the madness reads as a pirate doing their job too well, not as
something bolted on.

**Where each column comes from (Rex's personality matrix, `Writing/Cognitive Function Diagnostic/personality
matrix/profiles/`):**

- **Psycho core** = the type's "when meaning is lost" sentence (PERSONIFICATIONS.md): the lead pair keeps running
  with the inferior gone dark. That is the type at its extreme, and that is the killer.
- **Madness break** = how the inferior erupts (RULES.md, the slot table). This is what the madness meter (§7) does
  when it crosses the line.
- **Form** = the personification. Confirmed forms are Rex's (ISTJ golem, ISTP dragonfly, ISFJ ghost, ESFP alien);
  the rest are the unconfirmed candidates from PERSONIFICATIONS.md and can change.
- **Pirate archetype, ship job, why piracy allows it, madness triggers, the he/she hooks** = proposed, for Rex to
  keep, change or throw out. Names are Rex's; none are given.

**Rex's notes (2026-10-26):** this draft reads the abstractions (the forms) too literally; Rex will fix that, so
treat them as loose reference, not a script. Confirmed: **ISFJ is the medic who keeps you from leaving; ISTJ
follows the rules to the letter.**

## Each type in full

### ISTJ: the keeper of the articles (Si-Te, inferior Ne): confirmed, follows the rules to the letter

- **Psycho core:** keeps executing the command with no sense of whether it still applies. The articles say
  marooning for theft, so it is marooning, even for a crumb.
- **Why piracy allows it:** real pirate articles set the punishments (marooning, "Moses's law" of 39 lashes).
  The bosun carrying them out is doing the job.
- **Madness break (Ne erupts):** total character change or a chaotic leap: tears up the articles and leads a
  mutiny overnight.
- **Triggers:** rules broken, the captain contradicting the articles, the old way mocked.
- **He / she:** he was a navy master-at-arms who deserted with the rulebook; she wrote the articles of a crew
  that's all dead now and still enforces them.

### ISFJ: the surgeon who won't let you go (Si-Fe, inferior Ne): confirmed, the medic keeping you from leaving

- **Psycho core (the ghost):** keeps caring for people the old way with no sense of care taking another form.
  You are kept below, dosed, bandaged, safe, whether you're hurt or not.
- **Why piracy allows it:** ship's surgeons sawed limbs and dosed laudanum; nobody questions the sick bay.
- **Madness break (Ne erupts):** the ghost learns it's over: a total turn, from tender to vengeful.
- **Triggers:** someone leaving the crew, routines changed, being replaced at your side.
- **He / she:** he still sets a place at the mess for a dead crew; she poisons anyone who might take you away.

### ESTJ: the quartermaster (Te-Si, inferior Fi)

- **Psycho core:** keeps the system running efficiently with no sense of whether it's right. Captives are cargo,
  shirkers are a cost, and costs are cut.
- **Why piracy allows it:** the quartermaster was the elected power on a pirate ship: split the plunder, judged
  disputes, punished.
- **Madness break (Fi erupts):** an overwhelming crisis of worth: the ledger stops balancing and neither do they.
- **Triggers:** waste, losses, being called heartless.
- **He / she:** he sells prisoners by weight; she runs the shares so fairly that the unfair ones go overboard.

### ESFJ: the mother of the mess (Fe-Si, inferior Ti)

- **Psycho core:** keeps upholding the group's customs with no sense of whether they're logical. Our crew eats;
  other ships are food.
- **Why piracy allows it:** a pirate crew is a family that lives off everyone else's ships.
- **Madness break (Ti erupts):** sudden, harsh, rigid judgment: a cold verdict that someone must die for the
  crew, delivered as self-evident.
- **Triggers:** outsiders, a split in the crew, the customs disrespected.
- **He / she:** he runs the crossing-the-line ceremony a bit too hard; she has a stew no one asks about.

### ISTP: the boarder (Ti-Se, inferior Fe)

- **Psycho core (the dragonfly):** keeps refining and running its system with no sense of anyone it's for. The
  kill is computed to the exact point and done in half a second.
- **Why piracy allows it:** someone has to go over the rail first.
- **Madness break (Fe erupts):** raw outbursts and a desperate need for approval: suddenly needs you, badly.
- **Triggers:** being forced into crowds or feelings talk, their method doubted.
- **He / she:** he counts every cut; she has never once spoken during a fight.

### ISFP: the figurehead carver (Fi-Se, inferior Te)

- **Psycho core:** keeps expressing its truth in the moment with no sense of whether it achieves anything. The
  carvings are beautiful, and some of them were people.
- **Why piracy allows it:** pirates kept trophies and wore their art on their skin; the carpenter works alone.
- **Madness break (Te erupts):** cold, ruthless judgment by metrics: everyone is scored, and low scores go.
- **Triggers:** their work judged or destroyed, being made to conform.
- **He / she:** he carves the figurehead in the face of whoever he loves; she tattoos the crew and won't say what
  the ink is.

### ESTP: the gunner (Se-Ti, inferior Ni)

- **Psycho core (wildfire):** keeps overwhelming the present with no sense of where it leads. One more broadside,
  then the port too.
- **Why piracy allows it:** sacking ports and firing on ships is the trade.
- **Madness break (Ni erupts):** doom or sudden total certainty: "we all die today," then acts on it.
- **Triggers:** calm seas, no fights, being held back.
- **He / she:** he arm-wrestles for the right to light the fuse; she names every cannon and loves them more than
  people.

### ESFP: the castaway (Se-Fi, inferior Ni)

- **Psycho core (the alien):** keeps living each moment for how it feels with no sense of tomorrow, remembering
  only faintly where they came from.
- **Why piracy allows it:** Bartholomew Roberts' line about the pirate life, "a merry life and a short one."
- **Madness break (Ni erupts):** doom or sudden total certainty that there is no tomorrow, so nothing matters.
- **Triggers:** boredom, being made to plan, questions about home.
- **He / she:** he was found marooned laughing on a sandbar; she climbs the mast in storms for fun.

### INTJ: the navigator (Ni-Te, inferior Se)

- **Psycho core:** keeps executing the vision with no sense of the ground having changed. The course is set for
  the island on the map, through storm and crew alike.
- **Why piracy allows it:** treasure maps and legendary islands are what pirates chase.
- **Madness break (Se erupts):** animal force, a total break from fate: the cold planner goes berserk.
- **Triggers:** the course changed by someone else, the plan disrupted.
- **He / she:** he has charted where each of the crew will die; she will reach that island if she's the only one
  aboard.

### INFJ: the wrecker (Ni-Fe, inferior Se)

- **Psycho core (the lighthouse):** keeps guiding people toward the vision with no sense of what's actually in
  front of them. A false light on the rocks, for everyone's good.
- **Why piracy allows it:** wreckers lured ships aground with false lights and stripped them.
- **Madness break (Se erupts):** animal force, a total break from fate.
- **Triggers:** the crew straying from what they've foreseen, the vision betrayed.
- **He / she:** he sees sails from the crow's nest no one else can; she already knows how you two end.

### ENTJ: the rival captain (Te-Ni, inferior Fi)

- **Psycho core (the Juggernaut):** keeps driving the strategy with no sense of what it costs anyone. Joins your
  crew because a fleet needs a flagship.
- **Why piracy allows it:** pirate commodores built fleets and pirate republics.
- **Madness break (Fi erupts):** an overwhelming crisis of worth when their people act on their own feelings.
- **Triggers:** insubordination, crew choosing feelings over orders, losing.
- **He / she:** he sank his own ship to join yours; she plans to be captain by the third voyage.

### ENFJ: the shantyman (Fe-Ni, inferior Ti)

- **Psycho core (the siren song):** keeps leading people toward the vision with no sense of whether it's sound.
  The whole crew sings along, all the way onto the rocks.
- **Why piracy allows it:** the shantyman set the work and the mood; recruiters filled crews in port taverns.
- **Madness break (Ti erupts):** sudden, harsh, rigid judgment: the warm voice turns into a cold sentence.
- **Triggers:** the crew doubting, being contradicted with logic.
- **He / she:** he recruits in every tavern and none of them remember agreeing; she sings the dying to sleep.

### INTP: the powder master (Ti-Ne, inferior Fe)

- **Psycho core (the spider):** keeps building and exploring the system with no sense of anyone it's for. The
  new mixture needs testing, and the prisoners are right there.
- **Why piracy allows it:** gunpowder, fire ships and grenadoes were pirate tools.
- **Madness break (Fe erupts):** raw outbursts and a desperate need for approval.
- **Triggers:** ideas dismissed, being forced to socialise.
- **He / she:** he has rigged the whole ship to blow and calls it insurance; she builds traps no one asked for.

### INFP: the captive who stayed (Fi-Ne, inferior Te)

- **Psycho core (the moth and the flame):** keeps holding its ideal and imagining paths with no sense of whether
  any is achievable. Taken from a prize, they decided they were born to be a legend of the sea.
- **Why piracy allows it:** captives did sign the articles and join.
- **Madness break (Te erupts):** cold, ruthless judgment by metrics.
- **Triggers:** their ideal mocked, being treated as ordinary.
- **He / she:** he writes the crew's legend in the log and makes it true; she is becoming her truest self one
  victim at a time.

### ENTP: the card sharp (Ne-Ti, inferior Si)

- **Psycho core (slime mold):** keeps generating possibilities with no anchor in what has been proven. Every game
  is rigged a new way, every dare a little deadlier.
- **Why piracy allows it:** the den is contraband anyway; cheats were a fact of every tavern.
- **Madness break (Si erupts):** a sudden rigid lock into one identity or routine, or one old grudge.
- **Triggers:** repetition, being caught out, losing to someone they look down on.
- **He / she:** he bets crew mates' lives on dice for fun; she runs a con on you she started before you met.

### ENFP: the turncoat (Ne-Fi, inferior Si)

- **Psycho core (the butterfly):** keeps chasing possibilities that feel meaningful with no anchor to anything
  kept. Has sailed under every flag and burned every bridge.
- **Why piracy allows it:** privateers turned pirate, and pirates took pardons and turned hunter.
- **Madness break (Si erupts):** a sudden rigid lock onto one identity or one person, held with total rigidity
  (the romance's danger: that person can be you).
- **Triggers:** being tied down, or you pulling away.
- **He / she:** he's charming in every port's language; she has decided you are her one fixed point.
