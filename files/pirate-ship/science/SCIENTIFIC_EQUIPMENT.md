# Scientific equipment: the capability catalogue (body-and-world keeper, 2026-10-29)

Rex's rule (2026-10-29): **the limiting factor is the tools, not the chemicals.** Research all chemistry
equipment from then until now, make an inventory with dates and requirements for fuel and power, so the
world can be configured without restricting it later, and so a time-anomaly piece of equipment can be
added and its capabilities used at once without rebuilding the chemistry. ChatGPT (outside AI) added the
component idea: a burner heats, a vessel holds, a condenser cools, a pump changes pressure, an instrument
measures, and the simulation decides what an assembly can do.

**Ownership.** This file: the capability roles, the catalogue (seed here; a belt research lane completes
it), and the assembly pipeline. `TECHNOLOGY.md` says which items a setting makes available.
`CHEMISTRY.md` says what a process needs. The skill minigames (Rex's) decide how well the character runs
it. `../economy/GOODS.md`'s facilities list (the still, the forge, the furnace, the chymist's laboratory)
is the period's instance of this catalogue and stays the economy's.

**How to read.** **[science]** / **[history]** / **[model]** / **[fiction]**; **(Rex, date)**,
**(proposed)**, **(proposed, outside AI)**, **(open)**. Tables from `../body/data/equipment.json`.

- **[model]** A process (a `CHEMISTRY.md` reaction) asks for roles with numbers: heat to 500 °C held
  within 20 for an hour; a vessel of two litres that survives acid; a condenser for a vapour at 78 °C.

  process runs if every role it needs is covered. Equipment never knows the year.
